🛠️ Installation Tips

During installation, you might see a Windows SmartScreen notification. If you do, just click "More Info" and then "Run Anyway". This is completely normal and just Windows checking the software. The app is safe to run and contains no harmful code.

After installation, look for the dynamic-island icon in your system tray (bottom right corner). Right-click it to customize settings, change appearance, or temporarily disable the island. You can configure everything to your liking!
